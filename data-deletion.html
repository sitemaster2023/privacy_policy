# SiteMaster Data Deletion Instructions Last updated: Oct 24, 2026 You can permanently delete your SiteMaster account and all associated data at any time, directly from the SiteMaster web application. This page explains how. ## What gets deleted Deleting your account is **permanent and cannot be undone**. When you delete your account: - Your account and profile information are permanently deleted - All your projects will be permanently deleted - All team members will lose access - Your subscription will be cancelled immediately - Historical data cannot be recovered ## Before you begin - **If you are a member of an organization (and not its owner):** you cannot delete your account while you are still part of that organization. Leave the organization, or ask an owner/admin to remove you from it, and then follow the steps below. - Make sure you know your current account password. You will need it to confirm the deletion. ## How to delete your account 1. Sign in to the SiteMaster web application at [https://login.sitemaster.ai](https://login.sitemaster.ai). 2. Open **Settings**. You can click the edit (pencil) icon next to your name on your profile card, or go directly to [https://login.sitemaster.ai/dashboard/settings?section=danger](https://login.sitemaster.ai/dashboard/settings?section=danger). 3. In the Settings menu on the left, select **Danger Zone**. 4. Under **Delete Account**, enter your current password in the **Confirm your password** field and click **Verify**. 5. Once your password is verified, type **DELETE** (in capital letters) in the **Type "DELETE" to confirm** field. 6. Click **Delete My Account**. 7. In the final confirmation dialog, click **Yes, Delete Everything**. Your account will be deleted and you will be signed out automatically. ## Can't access your account? If you are unable to sign in, or you would like us to delete your data for you, email us at **contact@sitemaster.ai** from the email address registered with your SiteMaster account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will verify your request and process the deletion. ## Data we may retain We may retain certain information where we have a legal obligation or lawful basis to do so (for example, billing records required for tax and accounting purposes), as described in our [Privacy Policy](index.html). ## Contact Us If you have any questions about deleting your data, you can contact us: - By email: contact@sitemaster.ai - By visiting our website: [https://www.sitemaster.ai/](https://www.sitemaster.ai/) --- [Privacy Policy](index.html) · [Terms of Use](terms-of-use.html)
